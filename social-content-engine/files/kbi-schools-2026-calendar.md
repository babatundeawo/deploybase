# KBI Schools: Rolling Content Calendar (5 October 2026 to 31 December 2027)

> **How to use.** This is a secondary guide. Priority order: (1) public holiday or national event, (2) Oyo State school calendar event, (3) live trend that fits, (4) weekly theme below, (5) daily pillar. Always re-verify dates live. Islamic holiday dates are tentative until announced. Awareness day dates are the usual dates and must be confirmed in research.
