# Techbase Consultant Services: Rolling Content Calendar (5 October 2026 to 31 December 2027)

> **How to use.** Secondary guide only. Live research (new tools, news, trends from the last 48 hours) always wins when it fits Techbase's services. Awareness day dates are the usual dates and must be confirmed in research. School holiday weeks follow the Oyo State calendar, because most Techbase learners are in school.
>
> **Service codes:** COD coding, ROB robotics, AI AI literacy, WEB web development, TRN tech training, ITC IT consultation, CAC business registration.
> **Funnel:** AWR awareness, TRU trust, ENR enquiry or enrolment.

## Daily pillar rhythm

Monday AI Literacy, Tuesday Code Tuesday (one small practical skill), Wednesday Robotics Wednesday, Thursday Web and Business, Friday Build Friday (student project or demo), Saturday Parent Corner, Sunday Careers and Reflection.

## Recurring series (rotate through the year)

- **One Skill in 5 Slides:** a single concept explained as a carousel (Python loop, HTML tag, a sensor).
- **Ask Techbase:** answer a real parent, teacher or business question.
- **Build Friday:** a student made project explained step by step (never show or name a child without consent).
- **Plain English Tech:** a jargon term explained simply.
- **Small Business Starter Kit:** CAC registration, simple website, business email, basic cyber safety.
