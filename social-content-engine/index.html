Overview

One prompt, two fully researched, publish ready content sets

Give it TODAY or a specific date, and it resolves the day, researches a distinct live theme for each brand, previews both themes, then builds the complete platform by platform content set on request, British English throughout and zero hyphens anywhere.

Techbase Consultant Services

Forward looking, youth empowering STEM content. Every post anchors in coding, robotics, AI literacy, web development or IT training, and connects back to Nigerian youth, schools, or businesses. Never generic lifestyle tech.

KBI Schools

Warm, encouraging, community driven content for a school brand: academic development, parenting in the school context, youth wellbeing, and the Nigerian school calendar, written like a trusted educator speaking to a school family.

Visual format selection engine

Before any image prompt is written, one of ten visual formats is chosen and justified, from Instagram carousels to quote cards to before and after splits, matched to what the day’s theme actually needs.

Editorial integrity

Live research only, never outdated claims. Human, relatable, Nigerian conversational style. A trending topic or breaking discussion always overrides the scheduled theme.

Session flow

From a date to two finished .docx files

01

Resolve the date

TODAY resolves to the real current date at generation time. A specific date overrides it entirely, and gets locked across every step for both companies.

02

Research both themes live

Holidays, awareness days, trending education topics, and Nigerian school calendar events for KBI Schools. Trending AI, coding, robotics, and STEM news refracted through Techbase’s mission.

03

Preview and choose

Both themes are shown with their source context before any content is written, and the session waits for A or B before generating.

04

Generate, then offer the second brand

After the first company’s full content set and .docx are complete, it asks Y or N before touching the second, so the two never blend.
