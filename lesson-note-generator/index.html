Overview

One Project, every subject and class you teach

State the Subject, Class and Week number, the Term is optional. Claude works out which materials apply, fetches your state's official school calendar to compute the real dates for that week, including mid-term breaks, and writes the full booklet.

Use whichever you have

Mode A, e-note mode

You already have full e-notes with lesson content, definitions, examples, diagrams and exercises. Claude draws primarily from your e-note, then the optional textbook, then credible online sources.

Use whichever you have

Mode B, topic-list mode

You only have a weekly topic list, subject, class, session, topics, no content. Claude builds the full lesson content from credible online educational sources and standard textbooks.

Mode detection is automatic and applies per Subject and Class. One subject can use Mode A while another uses Mode B, inside the very same Project.

How the dates are computed

Real dates, fetched fresh every time, never hardcoded

01

Find the resumption date

Claude identifies the official resumption date for the relevant term, the Monday of Week 1, from your state's Ministry of Education calendar.

02

Compute the week

Week n Monday = Term Resumption Monday + (n minus 1) × 7 days. Week n Friday = Week n Monday + 4 days.

03

Adjust for breaks and holidays

A mid-term break on the Thursday or Friday shortens that week to Monday through Wednesday. A break covering the whole week re-maps to the first teaching week after it. Public holidays are noted, and notes are still generated for the remaining teaching days.

Inside every period

Nine parts, every single time

Behavioural Objectives

3 to 5 clear, measurable objectives.

Entry Behaviour

Prior knowledge, made relatable to your students.

Instructional Materials

Real or improvised materials, as a genuine list.

Reference Materials

Textbooks and page ranges, or a credible online source.

Introduction

Oral questions linking prior knowledge to the topic.

Presentation

Minimum 4 steps, each a Teacher's To-Do plus a full notebook-style Students' Note.

Evaluation

3 to 5 written questions, answered and corrected in class.

Conclusion

A summary plus 2 to 3 oral revision questions.

Assignment

One or more board-ready take-home tasks, listed, never blended into a paragraph.

Presentation steps read like genuine notebook pages, never a single thin sentence. Periods run one after another with no page break, only a thin rule line separates them, and content never repeats within the same week.

Get this tool

Set it up in three steps

01

Create a Project

One Project dedicated to lesson notes, named something like “Lesson Note Generator.” It can hold materials for every subject and class you need.

02

Edit three placeholders, then paste

Before pasting the instructions below into your Project's instructions field, replace {SCHOOL_NAME} with your school's full official name, {STATE} with the Nigerian state whose school calendar should be fetched, and {LOCATION_CONTEXT} with a short description of your students' everyday environment.

03

Upload your materials

Either three e-notes (1st, 2nd, 3rd term) or three weekly topic lists, per subject and class, whichever mode fits what you already have.
